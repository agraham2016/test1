Programs

Daytime, after-school, and weekend pathways

Flexible offerings invite learners to explore academics, wellness, arts, and leadership with caring mentors.

Morning Launch

Grades K-5

Daily academic coaching, literacy sprints, and STEM explorations that spark curiosity.

  • Breakfast clubs and mindfulness warm-ups
  • Small-group tutoring and learning labs
  • Weekly family feedback loops

After-School Studio

Grades 3-8

Project-based learning with arts, technology, and community service tracks.

  • Maker carts, coding workshops, and improv labs
  • Healthy snacks plus movement and yoga breaks
  • Quarterly showcases with family and neighbors

Weekend Curiosity Camps

All ages

Thematic Saturdays that pair field trips, guest artists, and nature walks.

  • Rotating themes: urban farming, robotics, storytelling
  • Sliding-scale tickets with sibling discounts
  • Transportation support where needed

Caregiver Circles

Parents & guardians

Workshops on advocacy, wellness, and financial readiness with trusted partners.

  • Monthly gatherings and resource exchanges
  • Childcare and dinner provided
  • Peer-led coaching with staff facilitators
